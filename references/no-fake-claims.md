# No Fake Claims Rule

**Personal brand depends on credibility. One invented metric = trust gone.**

## Forbidden

- ❌ Invented client outcomes ("got a client 300% more leads") unless real + verifiable
- ❌ Made-up case-study numbers ($1M ARR, 10K users) when actual is smaller
- ❌ Fake testimonials or quote attributions
- ❌ "Our team of X" when team is solo
- ❌ Internal-system names that don't exist (don't invent product features)
- ❌ Time-on-task numbers ("did this in 4 minutes") unless actually timed
- ❌ Implied scale ("hundreds of clients") without proof

## Allowed (real)

- ✓ Real shipped builds (Vow Sanctuary, GutReno, Wellness DNA, etc — see MEMORY.md)
- ✓ Real workflows (Jarvis stack, n8n flows, Obsidian + MCP)
- ✓ Real numbers from your projects (verifiable in code/repo/analytics)
- ✓ Real frameworks you actually use
- ✓ Hypothetical examples clearly marked ("imagine if...", "let's say...")
- ✓ Industry stats with linked source

## When in doubt

If a number sounds good but you can't trace it to a screenshot, repo, analytics dashboard, or client invoice — **drop it**. Replace with:
- A qualitative statement ("noticeable speed improvement")
- A verifiable smaller claim
- Remove the line entirely

## Per-platform sensitivity

| Platform | Sensitivity |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | HIGH — claims live forever, screenshotted, scrutinized |
| Twitter/X | HIGH — public, easily fact-checked, ratio risk |
| Facebook | MEDIUM — friends-of-friends notice |
| Instagram | LOWER — but still no |
| Pinterest | LOW — but consistency matters |

## Pre-publish gate

For every post, ask:
1. Can I screenshot/link the proof if challenged?
2. Would I say this number on a sales call to a real prospect?
3. Is this from MEMORY.md / a real shipped project?

If any answer = no, edit before scheduling.
