# Brand Split — Personal vs SkynetLabs

Two voices. Don't mix.

## Personal (`@waseemnasir2k26`, personal FB, personal IG)

**Voice:** human, casual, motivational, builder-in-public.
**Content mix:**
- Motivation + lesson ("Today I shipped X, here's what I learned")
- Behind-the-scenes ("late-night debugging, anyone else still up?")
- Casual question ("what tool changed your workflow this year?")
- Personal wins (NOT humble-brag — actual stories)

**No:**
- Hard sell / pricing
- Client case-study walls
- "Hire me" CTAs
- Corporate gloss

## SkynetLabs (`@skynetjoe`, agency LinkedIn, SkynetLabs FB page)

**Voice:** confident, proof-led, results-focused.
**Content mix:**
- Real client wins (with permission)
- Tactical breakdowns ("how we shipped X in Y days")
- Stack reveals (Jarvis stack, n8n flows)
- Hard CTAs ("DM `JARVIS`", "book a call")
- Pricing-anchored language

**No:**
- Personal life
- Vague motivation
- Off-topic memes (unless on-brand cringe-philosopher style)

## Cross-posting rule

One topic, two voices. Don't blanket-paste.

| Topic | Personal version | SkynetLabs version |
|---|---|---|
| n8n SaaS-killer | "I axed my $500 SaaS bill last month" | "How SkynetLabs migrates clients off Zapier — case study" |

| Jarvis stack | "How I run a 1-man agency on 4 tools" | "The SkynetLabs delivery stack — productized for $X" |
