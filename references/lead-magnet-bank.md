## Variant strategy

Each keyword can serve **2-3 magnet variants** for A/B testing:

```
JARVIS-A: PDF (visual heavy, design-led)
JARVIS-B: Notion doc (interactive, link-rich)
JARVIS-C: 5-min Loom video walkthrough
```

ManyChat splits 33/33/33 → track which converts to email at highest rate after 100 hits.

## Magnet format ranking (from shipped data)

| Format | Open rate | Email-conversion | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| PDF (10-15 pages) | High (90%+) | Medium (30-40%) | High (design + copy) |
| Loom video (3-5 min) | Medium (60-70%) | High (50-60%) | Low (talk through) |
| Notion doc | High (80%) | Low (15-20%) | Low (link share) |
| Google Sheet | Medium (50%) | Medium (30%) | Low (template) |
| ZIP (code + docs) | High for devs (95%) | Very High (70%) | Medium (package) |

Loom + ZIP = highest conversion. PDF is "feels premium" but lower email yield.

## Adding a new keyword

1. Check this list for collision
2. Pick magnet format(s) — recommend 2 variants for A/B
3. Submit ManyChat WA template (24-72h Meta soak)
4. Wire IG / FB / WA flows in ManyChat
5. Set GHL tag on trigger
6. Update this table
7. Update `references/manychat-keyword-flow.md`
