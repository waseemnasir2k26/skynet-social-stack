# Recipe — Weekly Competitor Sweep

15 min. Every Sunday. Track what 5-10 peers shipped this week. Don't copy. **Learn the angles you're missing.**

## Why

If you don't watch competitors, you ship in a vacuum. Your hooks get stale. Their best post becomes your post 3 weeks late.

## Inputs

- 5-10 peer accounts to monitor (mix: same-niche, adjacent-niche, aspiration-tier)
- Native analytics (peer accounts you can see public engagement on)
- Optional: SocialBlade, Notify, Tweet Hunter for tracking

## Workflow (15 min)

### 1. Snapshot top posts (10 min)

For each peer:
- Open their profile (LI / IG / Twitter)
- Note their TOP 3 posts of the week (highest visible engagement)
- Screenshot or note:
  - Hook line
  - Format (carousel / single / video / text)
  - Aesthetic
  - Engagement (likes + comments + reposts)

### 2. Pattern-match (3 min)

What patterns showed up across multiple peers?
- A specific topic everyone covered (e.g. "Claude 4.7 launch")
- A specific hook structure
- A specific format (everyone shipping carousels?)
- A new visual style emerging

### 3. Save findings (2 min)

Append to `outputs/competitor-sweep/<YYYY-WW>.md`:

```markdown
# Competitor Sweep — Week 17 of 2026

## @tenfoldmarc
- Top post: "Stop hitting Claude Code limits" (carousel, 6 slides)
  - Engagement: 2.4K likes, 180 comments
  - Hook: "Stop X." (Tier S)
  - Aesthetic: cream+rust circuit
  - Note: same template all 6 slides — algo seems to LOVE consistency within carousel

## @ainewsbyJoseph
- Top post: 1M context news commentary (FB news + LI cross-post)
  - Engagement: 800 likes
  - Hook: "Wait — Anthropic just..." (Tier A)
  - Aesthetic: news-anchor breaking-bar
  - Note: same content reposted 3x, different platforms, all hit

## Patterns this week
- 4/5 peers covered Claude 4.7 launch
- 3/5 used "Stop X." or "Wait — X." hooks (high-tension openers winning)
- 5/5 saw lower engagement on Friday posts

## Angles I'm missing
- Reactive news commentary — I'm too slow on launches (>72h late)
- Brutalist quote cards (only @memepro shipping these, getting saves)

## Action items
- Set up news-alert for Anthropic + OpenAI announcements
- Build brutalist quote card next batch
- Pause Friday posts, redirect to Tue-Wed-Thu
```

## Tools (optional)

- **Notify.me** — RSS/X feed digester
- **Tweet Hunter** — saves top tweets, search by topic
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — peer post tracking
- **SocialBlade** — IG / YT engagement trends

If skipping tools: just bookmark the 5-10 profiles, scan Sunday morning.

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ **Copy a peer's exact post.** Algo flags duplicate. Audience notices.
- ❌ **Sweep daily** — too noisy, weekly is plenty.
- ❌ **Sweep 30 peers** — too many = no insight. 5-10 max.
- ❌ **Skip recording** — without notes, you forget the angle by Monday.
- ❌ **Sweep but never ship** the missing angle. Sweep → ship within 2 weeks or sweep was waste.

## Compounding effect

3 months of sweeps = 12 weeks × 3 angles missed/week = 36 saved angles.

That's a year of content ideas grounded in what's actually working in your market.
